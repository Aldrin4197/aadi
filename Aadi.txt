Executive Logo Design Brief
Aadi
Your next space is here.
1. Brand Overview
Aadi is a property rental marketplace designed to connect landlords with people searching for spaces to rent. Through the application, landlords can list available properties, while tenants can browse, compare, and discover rental options that match their needs, location, lifestyle, and budget.
The platform may feature different types of rentable spaces, including bedspaces, boarding houses, apartments, residential houses, and commercial properties. By placing these options in one accessible platform, Aadi aims to make the rental search and listing process simpler, faster, and more convenient for both property owners and prospective tenants.
2. Brand Name
The name Aadi is short, memorable, and easy to pronounce. Its simplicity gives the brand flexibility and allows it to feel modern, welcoming, and suitable for a digital marketplace.
Rather than limiting the name to a single type of property, Aadi can represent the broader idea of finding a new space, beginning a new chapter, or discovering a place where someone can live, work, or grow.
The name should be presented with confidence and clarity so that it can become strongly associated with rental property discovery.
3. Tagline
The official tagline is:
Your next space is here.
The tagline communicates availability, discovery, and opportunity. It assures users that the space they are looking for may already be available through the Aadi platform.
The word space is intentionally broad. It may refer to a bedspace for a student, an apartment for a family, a house for long-term residence, or a commercial property for a business. This makes the tagline adaptable to the different property categories offered by the application.
4. Brand Purpose
Aadi exists to simplify the process of finding and offering rental properties. Many tenants rely on scattered social media posts, personal referrals, printed advertisements, or physical property visits to find available spaces. At the same time, landlords may struggle to reach suitable tenants or properly present their available properties.
Aadi brings both groups together in one organized digital platform. It allows landlords to present their properties clearly and gives tenants a more convenient way to search for available rental spaces.
The platform should reduce uncertainty, save time, and help users make more informed rental decisions.
5. Logo Objective
The logo should position Aadi as a trusted, accessible, and modern rental property platform. It should communicate the ideas of space, location, shelter, connection, discovery, and opportunity without appearing limited to only houses or apartments.
The visual identity should be welcoming enough for tenants while also appearing professional and dependable to landlords and property owners. It should feel suitable for both residential and commercial rentals.
The logo should be memorable, scalable, and recognizable across mobile application icons, websites, social media platforms, property listings, printed materials, and advertisements.
6. Central Brand Idea
The central brand idea is:
A space waiting to be discovered.
The identity should represent the connection between an available property and the person looking for it. Aadi acts as the bridge between landlords who have spaces to offer and tenants who need a place to live, stay, or operate a business.
The logo may visually suggest an open space, doorway, property outline, map location, or connection point. It should communicate that finding a suitable rental space is within reach.
7. Recommended Logo Direction
The recommended logo direction is a simple geometric symbol that combines the letter A with a property, doorway, map pin, or open-space concept.
The letter A may be shaped to resemble the roof of a house, the entrance of a building, or a directional marker. Negative space within the letter may form a doorway or opening, representing access to a new place.
Another possible direction is to create an abstract icon using two connected forms. One form may represent the landlord or property, while the other represents the tenant or searcher. Their connection may create a house, location pin, or stylized letter A.
The logo should avoid becoming too literal. A simple symbol will give Aadi a more distinctive and flexible identity than a detailed illustration of a house or building.
8. Symbolism
The visual identity may draw inspiration from doors, windows, roofs, buildings, location markers, keys, property boundaries, and open spaces. These elements can communicate rental discovery without requiring complex imagery.
A doorway can represent access, opportunity, and a new beginning. A location marker can represent property search and discovery. A roof or building outline can represent shelter and real estate. An open shape can communicate availability and welcome.
The symbol should ideally represent more than one of these ideas while remaining simple and easy to recognize.
9. Brand Personality
Aadi should feel trustworthy, welcoming, convenient, modern, helpful, and dependable. It should communicate that the platform is easy to use and designed to support both landlords and tenants.
The brand should feel friendly without becoming overly casual. It should also appear professional without feeling corporate, intimidating, or expensive.
Aadi should give users the impression that finding or listing a rental property can be straightforward and manageable.
10. Target Audience
Aadi is intended for individuals, families, students, professionals, entrepreneurs, landlords, property managers, and business owners.
Tenants may include students looking for affordable bedspaces or boarding houses, young professionals searching for apartments, families looking for homes, and entrepreneurs searching for commercial spaces.
Landlords may include individual property owners, boarding house operators, apartment owners, real estate managers, and commercial property owners.
The visual identity should appeal to a broad audience while remaining accessible to users with different levels of digital experience.
11. Visual Style
The visual style should be clean, modern, minimal, and approachable. The logo should use simple forms, balanced spacing, and clear proportions.
Rounded or slightly softened edges may be used to create a welcoming appearance. Geometric structures may provide a sense of organization, reliability, and professionalism.
The logo should feel contemporary and suitable for a technology-based marketplace, but it should not appear overly futuristic. It should maintain a strong connection to real places, communities, and everyday rental needs.
12. Color Direction
The color palette should communicate trust, accessibility, comfort, and opportunity.
Blue may be used to represent reliability, security, and professionalism. Teal or turquoise may suggest freshness, accessibility, and modern technology. Green may communicate availability, growth, and positive decisions. A warm accent such as coral, orange, or yellow may add energy and make the brand feel more welcoming.
A possible starting palette includes deep blue with the value #1E3A5F, modern teal with the value #1FA6A0, warm orange with the value #F59E42, soft off-white with the value #F7F8FA, and dark gray with the value #252A31.
The final colors should work well in both light and dark interfaces. The logo should also remain effective in black, white, grayscale, and single-color versions.
13. Typography Direction
The Aadi wordmark should use a clean and modern sans-serif typeface. The letters should feel friendly, legible, and confident.
Slightly rounded letterforms may help make the brand feel more approachable, while balanced spacing can communicate stability and professionalism.
The first letter A may be customized to reflect the shape used in the logo symbol. This can create a stronger connection between the icon and the wordmark.
The tagline should use a lighter or smaller type style so that it supports the brand name without competing with it.
14. Application Icon
The standalone application icon should remain simple and recognizable at small sizes. It should preferably use the letter A, a doorway, a location marker, or a combination of these elements.
The icon should not depend on the full wordmark or tagline to be understood. It should have a strong silhouette and remain clear when displayed on a mobile home screen, notification panel, map interface, or property listing.
The application icon should also work well with rounded-square mobile icon formats.
15. Design Applications
The logo should be designed for use across the Aadi mobile application, website, social media accounts, digital advertisements, property listing pages, landlord profiles, tenant profiles, onboarding screens, email communications, and promotional materials.
It may also appear on rental signs, property stickers, printed brochures, identification materials, and landlord verification documents.
The identity should remain consistent across digital and physical applications.
16. Elements to Avoid
The logo should avoid detailed illustrations of houses, apartment buildings, or city skylines because these may become unclear at small sizes. It should also avoid generic combinations of a house, key, and location pin unless these elements are presented in a distinctive and original way.
The identity should not appear exclusive to luxury real estate because Aadi includes affordable bedspaces, boarding houses, and everyday rental properties. It should also avoid looking like a construction company, architectural firm, hotel booking service, or property development corporation.
Overly complex linework, excessive gradients, thin details, and decorative fonts should be avoided. These may reduce clarity and make the logo less adaptable.
17. Desired User Impression
When users encounter the Aadi logo, they should feel that the platform is a reliable and convenient place to find or offer rental spaces.
Tenants should feel that the application can help them discover a place that suits their needs. Landlords should feel that Aadi provides a professional and accessible way to promote their properties and connect with potential renters.
The intended impression may be expressed through the statement:
“Aadi makes it easier to find the right space and connect with the right people.”
18. Brand Message
The primary brand message is:
Your next space is here.
This message should remain central to the visual identity and communication strategy.
Supporting brand messages may include:
Find a space that fits.
List your space. Reach the right tenant.
From search to space.
A better way to rent.
Spaces for living, working, and growing.
Connecting people with places.
19. Required Logo Deliverables
The final logo package should include a primary logo combining the symbol, Aadi wordmark, and tagline. It should also include a secondary logo without the tagline, horizontal and vertical arrangements, and a standalone application icon.
Full-color, monochrome, black, and white versions should be provided. Versions for light and dark backgrounds should also be included.
The final files should include scalable SVG formats and transparent PNG versions in different sizes. The package should also contain mobile application icons, favicons, official color values, typography specifications, minimum-size recommendations, clear-space guidelines, and basic instructions for correct logo usage.
Conclusion
The strongest creative direction for Aadi is a modern symbol that combines the letter A with an open doorway, property outline, or location marker. This approach would connect the brand name with the ideas of access, discovery, shelter, and finding a new space.
The final identity should be simple, inclusive, and memorable. It should represent more than property listings. It should represent the moment when someone discovers the place where their next chapter can begin.
